01 · The problem

Payroll review is still manual, fragmented and difficult to audit.

click

Read by hand

Payslips and employee documents arrive in inconsistent formats.

→ repetitive work across every payroll cycle
click

Checked in spreadsheets

Manual checks are difficult to reproduce later.

→ no stable record of the exact check and evidence
click

Held in one head

Critical knowledge often depends on specific payroll experts.

→ difficult to scale across employees and organizations
click

Or given to a chatbot

Which may produce confident answers without deterministic validation.

→ explanations alone are not a compliance engine

The result: slower payroll operations, inconsistent checks, and limited evidence when someone asks why.

02 · The insight

AI and deterministic validation solve different parts of the problem.

RULES ONLY

Cannot understand raw documents

  • Read a scanned or photographed slip
  • Rebuild an unfamiliar layout
  • Explain complex findings to employees and payroll teams
AI ONLY

Cannot own compliance decisions

  • Probabilistic output is not a stable compliance verdict
  • May hallucinate facts, rules or document fields
  • Cannot provide a deterministic audit result by itself

Conceptual comparison: probabilistic judgment versus deterministic validation.

AI-ONLY JUDGMENT
DETERMINISTIC ENGINE

AI extracts, investigates and explains — deterministic rules own the verdict.

04 · Why it's defensible

Reliable AI requires evidence, guardrails and measurable evaluation.

click

Honest partiality

An unverifiable field stays MISSING.

→ partial beats a plausible invented number
click

Source-bound answers

Legal answers are retrieved from an approved, versioned knowledge base.

→ legal RAG explains; deterministic rules remain the validation source of truth
click

Context from auth

Chat context is loaded server-side.

→ a browser-supplied employee ID is never trusted
click

AI usage is observable

Tokens, latency, failures and evaluation results are tracked across AI capabilities.

→ prompts and model behavior are governed through a versioned catalog
AI must not
  • Decide pass / fail
  • Approve a rule change
  • Trust a browser ID
  • Invent a source URL
05 · Stack & status

Production architecture with replaceable AI providers.

A modular platform with isolated domain logic. OCR, storage, authentication and LLM providers sit behind interfaces, so infrastructure can evolve without rewriting payroll rules.

Partial

Not claimed
